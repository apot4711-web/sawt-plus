Sawt+ logo concepts

Five flat directions for review. None of these is applied to the app yet. Pick one, ask for edits, or mix elements. The mark is shown on the dark and light surfaces used by the project.

A. Sad+ (ص+)

Arabic-first monogram: the letter Sad with a plus. The strongest local identity.

B. Plus Wave

Name-driven: a plus whose horizontal arm is an audio wave. No microphone.

C. Ripple+

A plus emitting two sound ripples: wireless, local, and minimal.

D. Mic+ (conservative)

An evolved microphone: no circle, flatter geometry, plus as the base. Keeps instant recognition.

E. S-Wave

An S-shaped sound ribbon with a plus. Global, distinctive, and close to the name Sawt.
